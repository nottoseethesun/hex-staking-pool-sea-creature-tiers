How It Works

Your T-Shares

A "HEX T-Share" is one trillion shares of the HEX Crypto Staking Pool. Find your total at plscharts.com/portfolio. Include every format you hold: HEX native stakes (sometimes called HEX solo stakes), HEX Stake Instances (HSIs), and "Pooled Stakes" — the generic term for the non-native, non-HSI formats, such as Maximus DAO tokens ($MAXI) and Pool Party tokens.

Where the Data Comes From

This app reads on-chain data directly from the HEX contract on Ethereum and PulseChain over JSON-RPC — no third-party indexers or APIs. It caches what it scans, so much may already be available locally (though it can be stale). The Update button fetches only the blocks added since the last saved cache, closing the gap to the current chain tip.

Scan & Re-Scan

This app reads HEX stakes straight from Ethereum and PulseChain over JSON-RPC, attributes every T-Share to its true holder, and excludes the Origin-Address (OA) funding cluster. It is read-only — it holds no keys and signs nothing.

Scan is the first, full build: it reads both chains from the HEX contract's deploy block, resolves wrapped stakes (HSIs and $MAXI), traces the OA cluster, and writes the report. It can take hours to days.

Re-Scan brings the report up to date by extending the saved data over only the new blocks — it never re-reads from the deploy block. Re-checking the OA cluster still sweeps a large set of wallets, so a refresh scales with the time since the last scan.

Tip: for a first build from GitHub, a recent full scan may be published there as a Release asset — hexleague seed installs it and skips the cold scan.
