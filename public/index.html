How It Works

Your T-Shares

A "HEX T-Share" is one trillion shares of the HEX Crypto Staking Pool. Find your total at plscharts.com/portfolio. Include every format you hold: HEX native stakes (sometimes called HEX solo stakes), HEX Stake Instances (HSIs), and "Pooled Stakes" — the generic term for the non-native, non-HSI formats, such as Maximus DAO tokens ($MAXI) and Pool Party tokens.

Where the Data Comes From

This app reads on-chain data directly from the HEX contract on Ethereum and PulseChain over JSON-RPC — no third-party indexers or APIs. It caches what it scans, so much may already be available locally (though it can be stale). The Update button fetches only the blocks added since the last saved cache, closing the gap to the current chain tip.
